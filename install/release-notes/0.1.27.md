# Rakumux 0.1.27

## A reply says when it didn't go in

- A reply from rakumux.com or the feed counts as delivered only once its agent's chat shows it as
  a new prompt. One whose Enter was lost, or that landed in a menu, now says it wasn't taken and
  may still be in the session's input box. Before, every reply counted as delivered 30 seconds
  after it was typed in.

## Sent once, however often you send it

- A reply written in the feed and sent again after an error (a time-out, say) is stored and typed
  in once. Before, the second try could put it in twice.
- Replies being typed in, and replies you write in the feed, no longer wait behind file downloads
  and AI titles.

## Upgrading

`U` installs the update and reloads the UI; the account service is replaced by the new version by
itself.
