# Rakumux 0.1.87

## The left column's tabs

- **All workspaces.** The view of every session is called All workspaces, in the workspaces menu,
  on the left column's tab and in the footer (All where that doesn't fit, as on a phone).
- **Each tab says its key:** `▴ All workspaces (w)   ≡ Inbox (f)   ♥  Heartbeats (B)`, where the
  column has room. `w` now shows the sessions too, from the Inbox or Heartbeats, with the menu of
  workspaces.
- Heartbeats' `♥` has a little more room before its name.
