# Rakumux 0.1.87

## The left column's tabs

- **All workspaces.** The view of every session is called All workspaces, in the workspaces menu,
  on the left column's tab and in the footer (All where that doesn't fit, as on a phone).
- **Each tab says its key:** `▴ All workspaces (w)   ≡ Inbox (f)   ♥  Heartbeats (B)`, where the
  column has room. `w` now shows the sessions too, from the Inbox or Heartbeats, with the menu of
  workspaces.
- Heartbeats' `♥` has a little more room before its name.

## A clearer Inbox

- **The selected card stands out.** As you move through the Inbox, the selected card is drawn with
  a heavy border all the way round, in the accent while the Inbox has the keys, so it's clear which
  one Enter, `r` and Space act on, in any terminal's colours.
- **A scrollbar.** When there are more cards than fit, a scrollbar at the right of the Inbox shows
  where the cards on screen sit among them all. A reply read whole that's longer than the pane has
  one too.
