# Rakumux 0.1.77

## Workspaces, Inbox and Beats

- The left column's first tab is now **Workspaces**. The workspaces' buttons moved there from the
  footer: they stay at the bottom of that tab, over the column's tabs, and switch which sessions it
  lists, as before. With many workspaces they wrap to up to three rows.
- The Inbox and Beats go across every workspace, so they show no workspace buttons.
- The footer names the session again. In the Tabs layout, which has no left column, the buttons are
  still in the footer.
