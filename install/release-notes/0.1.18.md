# Rakumux 0.1.18

## Whole chats in the Feed

- With the **Agent feed** on, Rakumux now keeps your agents' whole chats with your account, not
  just their final replies: your prompts, the agent's replies and visible reasoning, the tools it
  runs with their input, and the tools' output, from Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode. Private
  sessions never are, and keys, credential assignments and tokens are masked on your machine
  first. Kept on rakumux.com for 30 days.
- Select a reply in the Feed to read its whole turn in order: your prompt (`›`), the agent's
  reasoning, each tool call (`●`) with the first lines of its output, then the reply.
- OpenCode replies now appear in the Feed too.

## Settings

- A search field at the top: type to narrow Settings to the settings and keys you want. `Esc`
  clears it, then closes Settings.
- A new **Available on sign-in** section turns **AI summaries** and the **Agent feed** on and
  off. They're your account's switches, the same as on rakumux.com/features, so a change shows
  on every machine you're signed in on. Greyed out until you sign in.
- The "Session description content" setting is gone: the line under a session's name shows its
  AI summary, or your last message to its agent until it has one.

## Upgrading

The server changed: after `U` installs the update and reloads the UI, Rakumux says the server
lacks "whole chats in the agent feed" and offers to restart it in place (`y`/Enter; or press
`S`). Your sessions and their programs keep running. The AI status service replaces itself with
the new version.
