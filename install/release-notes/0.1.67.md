# Rakumux 0.1.67

## Workspaces in the footer

- The footer's left now holds your workspaces as tabs, each with its session count: **All**
  (every session), each workspace, and **Unassigned** (sessions in no workspace). Click one to
  show it. `⊞` opens the workspaces modal, and so does `+N` when the tabs don't all fit.
- All is where you start; the next start shows the tab you left.
- The workspaces modal lists All and Unassigned too.

## Rename and delete workspaces

- In the workspaces modal (`m`), `e` renames the workspace under the cursor and `d` twice deletes
  it; its sessions become Unassigned. The key line shows both, and they take a click.
- `rakumux workspace rename` renames one from the command line.
- On rakumux.com and in the app you can rename and delete workspaces too; each machine that has
  one carries it out only when a device you paired with it signed the change.
