# Rakumux 0.1.25

## AI session titles

- Signed in with the account's AI session titles on, each agent chat gives its session a short AI
  title, once its agent has written 5 messages or its turn ends. A name you gave (`r`) stays.
- `s`, or the `↻` after a hovered session's name, makes a new title; a spinner shows there while
  it's made. A title another session of the folder has gets ` 2`, ` 3`, …
- Titles replace AI descriptions: the line under each session's name is gone, and so is your last
  message shown there before one. `rakumux account status` still lists each session's description.
- AI session summaries are now called AI session titles, in Settings → Available on sign-in, the
  account dialog, Help and on rakumux.com. `rakumux account status --json` adds
  `ai_session_titles` and keeps `ai_session_summaries`.
- While the account's titles are on, local AI naming (your Anthropic or OpenAI key) steps aside.

## A tidier list

- Under Status, ready's `last hour`, `last day` and `older` headers and their sessions sit one
  column in from ready.

## Upgrading

`U` installs the update and reloads the UI; the account service is replaced by the new version by
itself. If your server predates 0.1.24, restart it in place too: press `S` in Rakumux mode, then
`y` (or run `rakumux daemon restart`). Your sessions keep running.
