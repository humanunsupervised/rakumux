# Rakumux 0.1.76

## Sessions, Inbox and Beats, in one place

- The left column has three tabs at its bottom: **Sessions**, **Inbox** and **Beats**. Sessions is
  the list as before, with Pinned, Status, Repos and Agent. The Inbox and Beats moved there from
  the drawer on the right, so the session keeps its width while you read them.
- Click or tap a tab, or press `f` (Inbox) and `B` (Beats); again, or `Esc`, goes back to the
  sessions. The Inbox tab counts new replies. `«` at the right of the tabs hides the column.
- On a phone they're the tabs at the bottom of the screen. The drawer on the right is rtop's (`t`).

## An Inbox that reads like one

- Replies are grouped by how long ago they came, under the headers ready sessions have: last hour,
  last 2 hours, and so on to older.
- Each starts with its time (`14:32 · 3m`, the date when it's older than a day), then its workspace
  in its own colour, then its session.
- The agent's answer is in full-strength text, and shows up to 10 lines; `▾ more` shows the rest.
  Short answers take only the room they need.
- A prompt with no answer yet shows a spinner and "agent is working on a reply".

## Make, rename and delete heartbeats in Beats

- **New heartbeat form.** Beats ends with a `+ New heartbeat` row: Enter, a click or a tap
  on it, or `n`, opens a form for the name, the task (several lines), the schedule (every so
  often, daily, or on chosen days), the folder and the agent. `Create` starts it on its schedule.
  `Ask agent` opens a session whose agent fills it in with you, as `n` used to.
- **Rename.** `c` renames a heartbeat. It stays on its schedule.
- **Buttons.** The selected heartbeat shows what you can do with it (run, pause or enable, rename,
  delete), and each one takes a click or a tap. Deleting still asks twice and keeps its runs.

## Workspaces

- **Unassigned** is now called **No workspace**.
- The footer's workspace tabs have a light `workspaces` label before them, when there's room.
- Enter on an Inbox card whose session is in a workspace that isn't shown goes into it (it said the
  session was gone).

## Search Help

- `/` in Help (`?`) searches every tab, action and setting: type `after`, or `rename session`, and
  read what Help says about it. Esc ends the search.
