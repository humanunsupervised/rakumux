# Rakumux 0.1.72

## Replies from rakumux.com and the app arrive on time

- What you send a machine from rakumux.com or the app (replies, new sessions, renames, moves) no
  longer waits behind slow work on that machine, such as downloading a reply's files or making
  AI titles. Before, a slow moment could hold it back by up to a minute or more.
