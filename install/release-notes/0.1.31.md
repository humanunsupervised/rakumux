# Rakumux 0.1.31

## Your Sprite in the Agent feed

- Signed in, `rakumux sprite connect` (or `N` the first time) now connects your Sprite to your
  Agent feed too. Its agents' replies, chats and sessions reach rakumux.com and your other
  computers whether this one is on or not, and the replies you write to them there are typed in.
- It asks once whether replies you write on rakumux.com are typed into the Sprite's agents
  (`--replies` or `--no-replies` answers for you).
- The Sprite never holds your sign-in: this computer enrols it, and rakumux.com gives it access of
  its own for 15 minutes at a time. `rakumux sprite disconnect`, or Revoke on rakumux.com's
  Features page, cuts it off.
- rakumux.com wakes a paused Sprite when you write to one of its sessions, and the Sprite stays up
  a minute after its agents finish, so their last replies leave.

## Upgrading

`U` installs the update and reloads the UI. For a Sprite you connected before, run
`rakumux sprite connect` again, signed in: it updates Rakumux there and connects its feed. The
server here hasn't changed in a way that matters off a Sprite, so there's nothing to restart.
