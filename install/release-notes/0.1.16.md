# Rakumux 0.1.16

## Choose the line under each session's name

- Settings → Session description content picks what the line under a session's name shows:
  **Your last message** (the new default), **Agent message** (0.1.15's line: the agent's latest
  reasoning, tool call or reply) or **AI summaries**.
- Your last message is what you last typed to the session's Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode,
  read from its own conversation. The agents' own insertions (reminders, notifications, command
  output) are left out.

## AI summaries follow each agent's turn

- With AI summaries chosen and your Rakumux account signed in, a session's summary describes its
  agent's current turn: your prompt and the agent's messages since (its reasoning, tool calls
  and replies), not the terminal's screen.
- While the agent works, the summary is written again every 5 of its messages, from your prompt
  and its newest 10. When it stops to wait for you, and at its final reply, it gets one at once;
  the final one reads your prompt and every message of the turn.
- Terminal output no longer triggers summaries for agents. Other programs (a shell, a build)
  are still summarized from their terminal.
- `sessions.ai_summary` in `config.json` changes both numbers:
  `{"every_messages": 5, "recent_messages": 10}`. The status service reads it every minute.

## Session list

- The column with the time and mark on the right is wider, so a long line keeps clear of it.
- Switching between the list's tabs with the arrow keys no longer shifts them.

## Upgrading

The server changed: after `U` installs the update and reloads the UI, Rakumux says the server
lacks "your last message in each session" and "AI summaries of each agent's turn" and offers to
restart it in place (`y`/Enter; or press `S`). Your sessions and their programs keep running.
The AI status service replaces itself with the new version.
