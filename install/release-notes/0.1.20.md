# Rakumux 0.1.20

## Pinned sessions on top

- Pinned sessions now have a section of their own at the top of the list, above every folder,
  app and status, under every tab. Drag them or use `J`/`K` to order them; that order is the same
  in every tab.

## Your sessions on rakumux.com

- With the Agent feed on, rakumux.com's new **Status** and **Folder** pages list your machines'
  sessions, grouped as the TUI groups them. Open one to read its conversation and write back to
  its agent.
- To do this, Rakumux sends rakumux.com each session's name, folder name, branch, agent and
  state. It never sends private sessions, and it stops when you turn the Agent feed off.

## Faster replies from rakumux.com

- Replies you write on rakumux.com reach your machine within about a second while you're using
  the site, or while a reply is waiting for that machine. The rest of the time, Rakumux checks
  for replies every 15 seconds.

## Upgrading

`U` installs the update and reloads the UI. The server didn't change, so there's nothing to
restart, and the AI status service replaces itself with the new version.
