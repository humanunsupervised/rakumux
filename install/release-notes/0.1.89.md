# Rakumux 0.1.89

## Heartbeats left waiting turn on

- A heartbeat an agent made or changed before 0.1.88 still waited for you to approve it, and the
  Heartbeats tab called it `◌ setting up`. The first start after updating turns on every such
  heartbeat that has its prompt, schedule and command. One that lacks any of them keeps waiting
  for its agent.
- This happens once. A heartbeat that `Ask agent` sets up after that still turns on when its
  agent saves the prompt and schedule.

## Inbox and Heartbeats have a header bar

- The Inbox and Heartbeats tabs now have a bar over them, like rtop's, that names them: `≡ Inbox`
  or `♥  Heartbeats`. It's in the accent colour while that tab has the keys. The cards and
  heartbeats start on the same row as the session list.
- When a reply or a heartbeat is open in full, the bar shows `‹` before the name, and clicking the
  bar goes back. The separate `‹` row is gone.
