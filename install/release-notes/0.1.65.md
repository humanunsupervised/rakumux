# Rakumux 0.1.65

## Workspaces

- Group your sessions into workspaces. The session list shows one workspace at a time, or the
  sessions in none (No workspace, where you start). The footer names the one shown; press `m` (or
  click it, or Settings → Workspace) to switch, add one, or delete one with `d` twice.
- `M` moves the selected session to another workspace, a new one, or No workspace.
- New sessions start in the workspace shown, and so do the sessions an agent starts from inside
  one. The next start shows the workspace you left.
- `rakumux workspace ls`, `add`, `rm` and `move` do the same from the command line.

## Workspaces on rakumux.com and in the app

- Your machines' workspaces show on rakumux.com and in the mobile app, under a switcher at the
  bottom. Move a session to another workspace from its menu; your machine carries it out only
  when a device you paired with it signed the move, like everything else asked there.
