# Rakumux 0.1.15

## What each agent is doing, under its name

- Every Claude Code, Codex and OpenCode session in the list now shows its agent's latest output
  under its name, live as the agent works: its reasoning, the tool it's running
  (`Bash: Run the tests`, `Edit: app/account.rs`) or its reply, on one line, in italics.
- The server reads it from the agent's own conversation on your machine: Claude Code's
  transcript (following a conversation into a background job too), Codex's rollout file, and
  OpenCode's database. Your prompts, tool output and subagents' messages are never shown.
- The line sits above the app and branch, and the lines under a name now stop before the
  time and mark at the right.

## Marks on the right

- Sessions without an agent (a shell, nvim, a build) show their status mark on the right after
  the time, like agents do. The space left of the name is the hover star's.

## AI descriptions

- The AI descriptions from 0.1.13 are hidden for now in favour of each agent's own line.
  Signing in still works, and they may come back.

## Upgrading

The server changed: after `U` installs the update and reloads the UI, Rakumux says the server
lacks "each agent's latest output" and offers to restart it in place (`y`/Enter; or press `S`).
Your sessions and their programs keep running.
