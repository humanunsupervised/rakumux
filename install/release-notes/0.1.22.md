# Rakumux 0.1.22

## Files with your messages

- Files you attach to a message or reply on rakumux.com now reach your agent. Once the agent is
  waiting for you, Rakumux downloads them into `~/.rakumux/uploads/` and types your message with
  each file's path on its own line, so the agent can open them.

## The Feed is a view of its own

- Press `f`, or the footer's `≡ Feed` button, to switch between your sessions and the Feed. `Esc`
  goes back to your sessions. The list's tabs are now Status, Folders and App.
- From session mode, a click on a card in the Feed opens it at once, as `Enter` does.

## More room for the session

- In Master detail, `« Hide list` at the foot of the list, or `b`, hides the list so the session
  takes the whole width. Click the rail on the left or press `b` again to bring it back.

## Steadier tabs

- Session tabs (the Tabs layout, and on phones) keep their places as sessions work and are read,
  so cycling through them reaches every session. When they don't all fit, the ends of the bar say
  how many are hidden.
- The list's tabs keep their names in a narrower list.

## Fixes

- On an account with more than 100 replies, Rakumux could skip replies waiting for this machine
  and never type them in. It now reads every page.
- A failed read no longer holds up telling rakumux.com that a reply was typed in, so it isn't
  marked undelivered.

## Upgrading

`U` installs the update and reloads the UI, and the AI status service replaces itself with the new
version. The server hasn't changed since 0.1.21: if you restarted it then, there's nothing to
restart. Otherwise press `S` once, so replies wait while you type. Your sessions keep running.
