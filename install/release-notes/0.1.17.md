# Rakumux 0.1.17

## The Feed

- A new **Feed** tab, first in the list's tabs: what your agents said when they finished a
  turn, from every machine signed in to your Rakumux account, newest first. Select a reply to
  read it whole on the right; Enter goes into its session when it runs on this machine.
- Off until you turn it on at rakumux.com/features (**Agent feed**). Then the status service
  sends each finished Claude Code and Codex reply to your account, never a private session's,
  with keys, credential assignments and tokens masked first. Replies are kept for 30 days.
  Turning the feed off or signing out empties it on this machine.
- OpenCode replies aren't in the feed yet.

## One AI summary per chat

- AI summaries no longer follow every 5 of an agent's messages. Each agent chat gets one
  automatically, once its agent has written 5 messages or finished sooner, from your prompt
  and those messages.
- After that, a summary is made only when you ask: hover a summary and click the ↻ at its end,
  or press `s` on the selected session. A spinner shows while it's being made.
- A summary stays through the session's changes (working, waiting, done) until you ask for a
  new one or its program ends.
- `sessions.ai_summary.every_messages` in `config.json` now sets when the automatic summary is
  made.

## Session list

- The tabs keep their place when you switch between them, the Feed included. On a phone the
  Feed shows as its mark alone, so the other tabs keep their names.

## Upgrading

The server changed: after `U` installs the update and reloads the UI, Rakumux says the server
lacks "the agent feed" and offers to restart it in place (`y`/Enter; or press `S`). Your
sessions and their programs keep running. The AI status service replaces itself with the new
version.
