# Rakumux 0.1.45

## Pinned tab

- Pinned sessions have a tab of their own, `★ Pinned`, after App: your pinned sessions alone, in
  the order you gave them (drag or `J`/`K` to change it). `←`/`→` reach it like the other tabs.
- Status, Folders and App no longer start with a pinned section: a pinned session is listed in
  its folder, app and status like any other, with a gold `★` beside its name. Click the `★` to
  unpin it, or press `p`.
