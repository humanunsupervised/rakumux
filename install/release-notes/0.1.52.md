# Rakumux 0.1.52

## Pinned sessions

- Pinned is back in the session list, as its first tab before Status. Press `*` to go there.
  Sessions are grouped by status, and `J`/`K` or a drag orders them within a status. Your saved
  order carries over. The drawer on the right now holds the Feed and rtop.
- With nothing pinned, the tab says `no pinned items`.

## Session list

- Under Repos and Agent, each repository's or agent's sessions are now grouped by status.
- Ready's `last hour`, `last 8 hours` and other time headers, and the new status headers, are now
  bars, quieter than the bars above them.
