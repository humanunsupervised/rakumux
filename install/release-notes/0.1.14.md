# Rakumux 0.1.14

## Sign in from the footer

- While you're signed out of your Rakumux account, the footer ends with **Sign in** at the
  bottom right in Rakumux mode. A tap asks for a sign-in code at once and shows the link and its
  QR code, as Settings → Account does.

## Pin with the mouse

- Point at a session in the list and a star shows just left of its name: `☆` pins it, the gold
  `★` unpins it. A click there doesn't change the selection, and `p` still pins the selected
  session. This needs a terminal that reports the pointer's movement, which desktop terminals
  do, over SSH too; on a phone, `p` pins.
- The list no longer shows a star after a pinned session's name: pinned sessions lead their
  folder, and the star shows when you point at one. Tabs and Grid cards still mark them with ★.

## Ready

- Under the Status tab, the replies you've read are now listed as **Ready** (they were Done).
- Pinned sessions lead Ready under their own ★ pinned header, however long ago you read them;
  the rest stay under last hour, last day and older.

## The footer

- In a session, the footer's way back reads `Ctrl-\ Rakumux mode`.

## Upgrading

Only the UI changed. `U` (or Settings → Update) installs it and reloads the UI; the server and
running sessions stay up.
